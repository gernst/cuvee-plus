\section*{Experimental setup}

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
The \UnboundedTasks{} tasks are C programs with the property unreach-call
(\texttt{benchmarks/sv-comp/c}, task set \texttt{C.unreach-call.Unbounded}). They are printed from the
Dafny translations of tasks of ReachSafety-Arrays and ReachSafety-Loops
(\texttt{benchmarks/sv-comp/dafny}), whose concrete loop bounds are replaced by symbolic ones, hence
unbounded, and keep the expected verdicts of the original tasks. Scripts patch the printed programs for
other verifiers: the SV-COMP harness, sizes for the arrays, below $2^{31}$ bytes, nondeterministic initial
values for the local variables, and, for 34 tasks that other verifiers rightly found false, bounds or
conditions on the inputs (see \texttt{benchmarks/sv-comp/c/README.md} and \texttt{false-verdicts.md}).
The runs of the other tools are those of October 10, 2026, before the initial values, the sizes and the
bounds; the runs of Cuvée dated October 11 are on the patched programs.

\paragraph{Tools.}
Cuvée runs with \texttt{-teq -simplify -split -simplify}: it generates trace equation systems for
the loops, simplifies them with its lemma database, and splits the remaining goals. Cuvée+Z3 adds
\texttt{-fallback -z3}, which lowers the goals that remain after simplification into first-order logic
and hands them to Z3. The lemma database of \texttt{-teq} consists of the modules core, arithmetic,
counters, aggregation, arrays and search; the variants marked full TEQ add the further modules nested,
sorting and strides (\texttt{-include teq:nested -include teq:sorting -include teq:strides}). Cuvée only
reports safe programs (verdict true); it does not search for counterexamples. The other tools are the
SV-COMP 2026 versions of Symbiotic, AISE, UAutomizer, CPAchecker and Korn, run with the options of their
official SV-COMP benchmark definitions.

\paragraph{Execution environment.}
All runs use BenchExec on a cluster of Intel Core i9-9900 machines (3.10\,GHz) with Ubuntu 24.04,
with a limit of \TimeLimit{} of CPU time, \MemoryLimit{} of memory and \Cores{} CPU cores per run,
unless the legend says otherwise, and the adaptations of the tools to our cluster described for the
runs on the categories of SV-COMP.

\paragraph{Scoring.}
Results are scored as in SV-COMP: $+2$ for a correct proof (true), $+1$ for a correct alarm
(false), $-32$ for a wrong proof and $-16$ for a wrong alarm; timeouts, crashes, and unknown results
count $0$. Our runs do not validate witnesses, so every correct verdict counts.

\paragraph{Plots.}
The
scatter plots compare the CPU times of the variants of Cuvée with and without the full lemma database
task by task, for the tasks that at least one of them solves correctly; tasks that only one solves lie
on the line labeled unsolved of the other. Dotted lines mark a factor of 10, dashed lines the time limit.
