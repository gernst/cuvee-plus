\section{Insights}\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{The method is lookup; the difficulty is the engine}
Verification by lookup states, once, what loops of a shape compute, and finds the loops of a program among the shapes.
On the MonoCera tests this proves within seconds what inference of quantified array invariants does not find in
fifteen minutes, and the price is that a loop without a lemma stays open. The examples of this document strained not
the method but the engine that performs the lookup. Each practice that a lemma writer would otherwise need is a
symptom of a missing capability of the engine, and the engine of Section~\ref{sec:engine} is organized by the
symptoms it removes:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.31\textwidth}p{0.62\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
capability & what a lemma would need without it \\
\midrule
congruence closure as the context & open variables for every trace of the goal, tied by equations; the whole atom of the
  postcondition as left-hand side, since a variable argument is not expanded; equal counters kept apart \\
closed conditions looked up & every iteration constraint as a condition restated with fresh lists, identified with the
  goal's by equations \\
a decision procedure for linear arithmetic & lemmas for the bounds of a midpoint; conditions stated in the exact form of
  the goal's facts, since only difference constraints are combined \\
canonical forms of literals & a higher-order variable pinned to $i - 1$ for every store position, a variable $d = -1$ for
  every downward step \\
views of cycles & per-variable equations, each recursive through the others, and every lemma tying the dependency lists
  back to their traces \\
patterns over whole cycles, with the constraint in the update & a lemma per pointer, unable to state that the
  pointers move together \\
forward facts with triggers & rules on atoms for facts that no atom mentions, and abstractions of constraints that hide
  them from later rules \\
suspension of the rule being applied & no condition may mention the left-hand side, so the exit of a loop cannot be
  stated about its own trace \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
What remains for the lemma writer is the algorithm's knowledge: what a loop nest computes, which invariant an outer
loop keeps, and the vocabulary in which the postcondition is stated.

\subsection{The representation: equations per component}
A loop over variables $\bar x$ is, semantically, one fold over its iterations with the state tuple as the
accumulator. A system of one equation per variable is this fold decomposed by projection: each equation steps one
variable, with the others among its dependencies. Where the variables form a layering (a counter, an index over it, an
array written at the index, a sum over the array), the decomposition is the compositional reasoning of the method: each
equation meets a small lemma of the database and the result is substituted downstream, which is why single loops and
the arrays of SV-COMP need nothing written for the program. A single fold over the whole state would first have to
rediscover that layering before any lemma on a counter or a sum could apply.

Where the variables read each other, the decomposition is the obstacle: the pointers of a merge, the bounds of a
binary search, the position and the array of gnome sort give equations that are individually meaningless, each
recursive through the others. The partition by strongly connected components takes the best of both: a variable in no
cycle keeps its own equation, where the database's lemmas apply, and a cycle becomes one trace of several
accumulators, which a lemma may name as a whole. The per-variable view of a component is not lost; it is computed on
demand, as a projection, when a lemma about a single accumulator needs it. The lemmas keep their form, and the
engine translates between the representations.

The same holds for nested loops. An inner loop described by fresh lists of the outer loop, one per inner trace and its
final value, puts the facts that belong together into different combinators, and every substitution between them is a
lemma. As a value of the outer update, the inner loop is one term, the lemmas of single loops apply to it, and only the
number of its iterations is a choice of the outer loop, to be determined by its exit.

\subsection{Soundness by totality}
A lemma that holds for the loops of programs is not yet a lemma: it is applied wherever its term occurs, also to
traces that no loop produced, e.g.\ a trace past its exit, whose steps are unspecified. The bisection shows the
failure and the remedy. Stated for a partial update, its bounds derive $\mathsf{false}$; stated for a total definition,
which keeps the state where the program's step is undefined, they hold for every run and have a proof. The engine
supports this by matching case lists in one direction: the program's guards are complete in the context of its
updates, by construction, so a lemma may be total where the program is partial. This turns a premise that every lemma
with guards would carry, and that no engine checks, into a case of the lemma's definition.

\subsection{What the open examples need}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Closed conditions looked up modulo unused and repeated dependencies}: the constraints of nested loops carry
  the counter of the iterations unused, so a summary of a sort must match them with open lists and identify these by
  equations, which is the bookkeeping that the lookup is meant to remove.
\item \emph{Views memoized for terms, not only for variables}: a condition that contains a trace is instantiated with
  fresh bound variables at every evaluation, and its views are recomputed; lemmas about cycles must name the trace by a
  rule variable and read its components through open variables, and the insertion sorts and the first duplicate,
  whose constraints contain traces as terms, are blocked by it.
\item \emph{Normal forms that keep facts}: $\intf(n) = x$ becomes $n = \natf(x)$ only under $0 \le x$, which is then lost;
  the unconditional form $0 \le x \wedge n = \natf(x)$ keeps it (the reversal).
\item \emph{Remainders as linear terms over quotients} in the arithmetic, which closes the strided writes of
  Section~\ref{sec:pr2} without a lemma for one form of an equation.
\item \emph{A more precise read relation}, taking the guards only for the results that differ between the cases, so that
  a counter of a guarded body stays outside the cycle of the variables that the guard reads; the partitions have a
  cycle of three accumulators for that reason, whose views are too expensive.
\item \emph{Lemma schemas}, with the number of stores, cases or dependencies as a parameter, in place of one lemma per
  arity.
\item \emph{Quantified left-hand sides or witnesses} for existential postconditions: the matcher has no case for
  quantifiers.
\item \emph{Diagnostics for non-termination}, which report the rule applications that repeat rather than the last one.
\item \emph{Proofs for the lemmas of programs}: definitions of the abstract predicates and functions of the sorts in
  Lean, as the database's lemmas have them, so that the summaries and step lemmas are checked rather than trusted.
\end{itemize}
