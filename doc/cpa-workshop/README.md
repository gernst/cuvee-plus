# Trace equations for program verification (CPAchecker Workshop 2026)

- `story.md`: the storyline, for an audience that knows nothing about TEQs: two worlds and what lies between
  ("routine, not trivial"), then the paper (`~/Papers/array-acceleration`) in its order: the three key ideas of its
  motivation, the database, the evaluation with its table and plots, the conclusion. The earlier storyline of three claims follows as a backup section.
- `review.md`: points from re-reading the story against the slides and the repository, and how they were addressed.
- `slides.tex`: the beamer deck that tells the story (16:10, 81 pages with overlays). Compile with
  `pdflatex slides.tex` (twice).

All slides are drawn in beamer and TikZ, in the look of the earlier decks (`~/Papers/array-acceleration/talks/CPA.pdf`
and `Next Generation.pdf`): Linux Libertine, code in Bera Mono, programs in boxes with callouts. The pictures in
`figures/` are taken from those decks:

- extracted: `photo.jpg`, `svcomp.jpg` (the SV-COMP plot), `aws.jpg`, `datadog.jpg`, `meme.jpg` (the code on its
  sheets is set on the slide), `checkmark.jpg`, `unicorns.png`, `wand.png`, `lean.png` (no longer used);
- rendered from the vector graphics of the title page: `logos.png`;
- copied from the paper's `results/`: `quantile-Arrays.pdf`, `quantile-Loops.pdf`, `rules-frequency.pdf`;
- generated by `scripts/experiments.py` (the same data): `quantile-Arrays-bottom.pdf`, `quantile-Loops-bottom.pdf`,
  the quantile plots with the legend below, used on the slide.

The results are those of the paper. The slide "How Cuvée is built" leaves a grey placeholder for the speaker's
own experience.
