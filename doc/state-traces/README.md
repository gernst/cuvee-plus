# Trace equation systems by two examples

`state-traces.tex` (and its PDF) explains the approach to a reader who knows verification but not trace equation
systems, by the array initialization and the swap of two arrays of [examples/dafny/state](../../examples/dafny/state)
under `-teq-state -prove`: the verification condition over traces, the lemma database, the proofs step by step from
the rewrite log, the engine in short, and what the examples do not show. Compile with `pdflatex state-traces.tex`
(twice). Written Oct 13, 2026, with the help of Claude Code (see the AI declaration in the document).
